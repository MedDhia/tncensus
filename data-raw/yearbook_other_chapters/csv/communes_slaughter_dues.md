## Columns

- `census`, `table_id`, `gallica_ark`, `source_page`, `gallica_view`, `year`: as in the other files (left page: beef to goat; right page: pig, horse, camel/other and the total)
- `commune`: as printed (footnote calls such as "(1)" kept)
- `animal`: `beef` (Bœuf), `veal` (Veau), `sheep` (Mouton et agneaux), `goat` (Chèvre [et chevreaux]), `pig` (Porc), `horse` (Chevaux et mulets), `other` (Autres; to sgt1925), `donkey` (Âne; from sgt1926), `camel` (Chameau); `all` for a total stored on its own (see below)
- `animal_fr`: column heading as printed in that edition (French)
- `measure`: `tariff_per_head` (*Tarif par tête*, francs), `sums_collected` (*Sommes perçues*, francs), `total_collected` (*Totaux*)
- `value`: francs; printed » = 0. Tariffs as printed ("0 15" = 0.15). A sum stored as whole francs without centimes (e.g. `680`) has its centimes hidden in the binding and could not be solved
- `text_fr`: text printed in place of a tariff or across the row (e.g. "0,10 par kg", "1,00-1,20", "0 fr. 06 par kilogr. de viande abattue")
- `flag`: empty; `not_applicable` (tariff printed as text, or cell covered by a text printed across the row: value NA, text in `text_fr`); `derived_from_totals` (sum whose centimes are hidden in the binding, solved as row total − other sums and checked against the printed francs); `total_mismatch` (printed value kept although the checks show it inconsistent, see the edition's section); `illegible`

The row *Totaux* is stored only for communes whose dues are not itemised by animal (all sums » or a text across the row, and the total printed: dues levied per kilogram, farmed out, or counted with another commune): `animal` = `all`, `measure` = `total_collected`. For the other communes it equals the sum of the animals and is used for the check only. The page totals (*A reporter*, *Report*, *Totaux*) are not stored.
