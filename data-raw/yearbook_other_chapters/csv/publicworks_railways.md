(exact) and the printed ratios (recette par km et par an, par km de train, produit moyen d'un voyageur / d'une tonne) against the quotients of the printed totals (to printed rounding, 1 % tolerance). The ratios printed with a different definition fail by design (e.g. the Medjerdah receipts per km).

Every failed check below was re-read on a zoomed native-resolution crop, or recurs unchanged in independent readings of several editions: they are printed errors (printed values kept). Ratio failures recur in every printing of the same year (e.g. Graïba-Gabès 1923 receipts per km 5.216 against 7.216 computed; mining branches 1921-1922 receipts per train-km), and are printed figures; total failures by 1 franc are rounding in the printed parts.
