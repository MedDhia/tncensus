## Columns

- `census`: source edition (`sgt1922` ... `sgt1935`)
- `table_id`: printed layout: `achour_roll` (sgt1926-sgt1930, one page per crop, européens and indigènes columns), `achour_nat` (sgt1935, Tunisia totals), or (sgt1922-sgt1925, yield-class tables) `achour_a` (a) européens) and `achour_b` (b) indigènes)
- `gallica_ark`: Gallica ark of the volume
- `source_page`: printed page number (for a two-page spread, the page the cell is printed on)
- `gallica_view`: Gallica view
- `year`: year of the roll: the edition year (sgt1922-sgt1930 print no year; sgt1935 prints "ACHOUR (1935)")
- `region`: région as printed, with its chef-lieu in brackets (sgt1925: "1re Région (Bizerte)" ... "5me Région (Sfax)", the chef-lieu printed in its own column; sgt1926-sgt1930: "1re Région (Bizerte)"); "Territoires Militaires du Sud"; empty when the table lists caïdats only (sgt1922-sgt1924)
- `controle_civil`: contrôle civil as printed (sgt1925-sgt1930); empty otherwise
- `caidat`: caïdat as printed (sgt1925: the caïdat of Cap-Bon, printed in two lines, as "Cap-Bon, Nabeul" and "Cap-Bon, Soliman"); `TOTAL (printed)` for the sgt1935 table, which prints only the totals for Tunisia
- `crop`: `wheat` (blé), `barley` (orge), `oats` (avoine), `broad_beans` (fèves), `chickpeas` (pois chiches), `maize` (maïs), `sorghum` (sorgho), `flax` (lin)
- `crop_fr`: crop as printed
- `roll`: `european`, `indigenous` (sgt1935: "par les tunisiens"), or `all` for the exempted new land of sgt1926-sgt1935, printed for both together
- `roll_fr`: printed table title or column head
- `yield_class`: yield class of the area, in hectolitres (sgt1922-sgt1925 only): `6_plus` (6 hectolitres and over), `5`, `4`, `3`, `2`, `1`, `under_1` (less than one hectolitre); empty for the exempted new land and where no yield class is printed
- `yield_class_fr`: printed column head (under "Nombre d'hectares d'un rendement de :")
- `measure`: `area_sown` (hectares sown, liable to the achour) or `area_new_land_exempt` (cultures effectuées sur terrains nouvellement défrichés et exemptées de l'achour)
- `measure_fr`: printed column head
- `unit`: `hectares`
- `value`: area in hectares (printed in hectares and ares, "h. a.": "7.912 09" = 7912.09 ha); printed » = 0
- `flag`: empty, or `derived_from_totals` (figure broken or not printed, solved as Récapitulation minus the other roll, or, sgt1922-sgt1925, as the line's TOTAL minus its other yield classes), `total_mismatch` (printed value kept; the line's Récapitulation and the column Totaux (sgt1922-sgt1925: also the line's TOTAL) require another value), `row_realigned` (printed one line too high or too low, stored on its own line), `not_applicable` (nothing printed in the cell, NA)

Printed layouts. sgt1922-sgt1925: for each of the eight crops three tables, a) Superficies ensemencées par des européens, b) par des indigènes, c) Récapitulation, by caïdat (sgt1922-sgt1924, one page each) or by région, chef-lieu de région, contrôle civil and caïdat (sgt1925, two-page spreads), with the columns Nombre d'hectares d'un rendement de : 6 hectol. et au-dessus, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1 hectol., moins d'un hectolitre, TOTAL, and Cultures effectuées sur terrains nouvellement défrichés et exemptées de l'achour. Stored: the yield classes and the exempted new land of tables a) and b); TOTAL (= the seven yield classes; the exempted land is outside it, e.g. sgt1922 blé européens L'Arad: TOTAL 6.50, new land 21.50), the c) tables and the Totaux lines are checks. sgt1926-sgt1930: one page per crop (a) Blé ... h) Lin), by région (chef-lieu in brackets), contrôle civil and caïdat, with the Nombre d'hectares ensemencés par les européens, par les indigènes, Récapitulation, and the exempted new land for Européens et Indigènes together (roll `all`); no yield classes. sgt1935: one page, "ACHOUR (1935)", the same four columns ("par les tunisiens") for the eight crops, Tunisia totals only (caïdat `TOTAL (printed)`).

Footnote (sgt1935), translated: (1) The figures of this column [exempted new land] are included in those of the preceding column, Récapitulation.
