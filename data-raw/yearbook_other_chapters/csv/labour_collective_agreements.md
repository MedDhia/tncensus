# Liste des conventions collectives rendues obligatoires par arrêtés au cours de l'année 1939 ; Sentences arbitrales ou accords rendus obligatoires en 1939

*Collective agreements made binding by order in 1939, and arbitration awards or agreements made binding in 1939.*

Source: *Statistique générale de la Tunisie*, chapter *Travail et prévoyance sociale*, edition sgt1939 (`bpt6k9105739w`), p. 166, f172 and p. 167, f173. Source gallica.bnf.fr / Ministère des Affaires étrangères.

Transcriptions: `../transcribed/labour_sgt1939.txt`; rebuild with `python3 data-raw/yearbook_other_chapters/extract_labour.py build`.

Rows: 14 (6 collective agreements, 8 awards or agreements). A text table: one row per printed line, every field as printed; no numeric values (value_type: text). Read on the page images (all fields).

## Columns

- `census`: edition the value is taken from (rule 4 series: the latest edition printing it; rule 5: the edition)
- `table_id`: transcription section
- `gallica_ark`: Gallica volume ark (views are numbered within a volume)
- `source_page`: printed page number (two-page spreads: the page the cell is printed on)
- `gallica_view`: Gallica view (fNNNN)
- `year`: year of the figures (HBM: last year of the printed period)
- `kind`: collective_agreement (convention collective made binding by an arrêté d'extension, decree of 4 August 1936), arbitration_award (sentence arbitrale, named by its arbitrators) or conciliation_agreement (accord signed during the compulsory conciliation and arbitration procedure, decree of 22 April 1937)
- `item_no`: order in the printed list
- `award_fr`: sentence or accord as printed (arbitrators' names)
- `order_date_fr`: date of the arrêté d'extension and of its publication in the J.O.T., as printed
- `conclusion_date_fr`: date of conclusion of the convention / date of the sentence or accord
- `industry_fr`: industry, trade or commerce as printed
- `scope_fr`: champ d'application
- `publication_date_fr`: date of publication in the J.O. (awards)

Printed in sgt1939 only. 'Industrie de 'ameublement' is kept as printed (missing l'). No totals: no checks.
