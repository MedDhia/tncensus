## Image versus OCR

Every section of every edition is marked `img: all` in the transcriptions: every value was read on native-resolution crops of the page image. The yearly Conservation foncière series (requisitions, value brackets, titles) of sgt1923-sgt1930, first drafted by carrying earlier years over from the previous edition, were then re-read in full, line by line, on crops of each printing (24 printings, 2.496 cells including the Totaux lines and columns): no cell differed from the transcription. The Gallica word OCR was used only as a cross-check (the 135 cells whose OCR token differed are all OCR misreadings).

## Coverage

Every table of the chapter is transcribed in every edition that prints it, every cell read on native-resolution crops of the page images (contact sheets of every view from the view before the chapter title to the next chapter title); the yearly Conservation foncière series of sgt1923-sgt1930 were re-read in full on each printing. Nothing in the chapter is left out: the views not listed as tables are the chapter title and blank pages. The boxes printed under a) (totals repeating the table) and the TOTAUX columns and lines are used as checks only; the récapitulation, which exists only as cumulative totals, is stored.
