## Image versus OCR

Every section of every edition is marked `img: all` in the transcriptions. For some printings the Gallica word OCR was used to lay out a draft, which was then corrected line by line against crops of the page image; the image is the source of every value. The overlapping years were read anew on each printing (not copied from another edition), so the overlap comparisons compare independent readings.

## Between Property and Posts

Between the Property chapter and Posts, sgt1922-sgt1935 print chapter VII Instruction publique (sgt1922 f113-f152; it is the public-instruction chapter already extracted elsewhere, not transcribed here); sgt1936 and sgt1939 print chapter VI Instruction publique between Propriété foncière and Postes (sgt1936 f68-f77, sgt1939 f65-f76).

## Coverage

Not transcribed: the exchange-rate tables ("c) Taux de change" / conversion rates applied to international money orders, dated rates by country, 13 to 34 pages per edition in sgt1922-sgt1930: sgt1922 f159-f171, sgt1923 f643-f661, sgt1924 f1133-f1156, sgt1925 f169-f197, sgt1926 f719-f752, sgt1927 f181-f214, sgt1928 f705-f738, sgt1929 f183-f216, sgt1930 f717-f750). They are lists of dated rates, not statistics of the service, and were left for a separate pass. Everything else in the chapter is transcribed in every edition that prints it, every cell read on native-resolution crops.
