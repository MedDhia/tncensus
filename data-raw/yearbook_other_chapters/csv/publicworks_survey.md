## Coverage

Every table of the chapter is transcribed in every edition that prints it, every cell read on native-resolution crops of the page images (whole-page views of every page from the chapter title to the next chapter title, then zoomed crops of every table). Cells cut by the binding are `illegible` (stored from another printing when one exists). Nothing printed in the chapter is left out; printed TOTAUX / TOTAL lines are used as checks only.
