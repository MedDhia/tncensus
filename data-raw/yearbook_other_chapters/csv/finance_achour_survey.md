# Achour: survey of every edition

The achour (tithe on cereals) tables of the *Finances* chapter, view by view. Views from `finance_survey.md`, confirmed on the page images. Extraction: `extract_finance_achour.py`.

- **sgt1922**: f188-f211 (pp. 164-187), three pages per crop (a) européens, b) indigènes, c) Récapitulation; Blé ... Lin), 31 caïdats as printed (Nefzaoua ... Djerid; Cap-Bon one caïdat; blé a) without Djerid) x 7 yield classes, TOTAL and exempted new land [transcribed, every cell read on the image]. Faulty cells (total_mismatch): blé a) Banlieue 6 hectol. and 3 hectol., Souk-el-Arba 2 hectol.; blé b) Ouled-Ayar 4 hectol.; avoine a) Zaghouan and avoine b) Tadjerouine moins d'un hectolitre; pois chiches b) Mateur 4 hectol.; lin b) Ouled-Aoun 6 hectol. Lin b): a figure printed one line too low (row_realigned). Several misprinted TOTAL, Totaux and c) figures.
- **sgt1923**: f678-f701 (pp. 176-199), same layout as sgt1922 (31 caïdats; blé a) without Djerid) [transcribed, every cell read on the image]. Faulty cells (total_mismatch): blé b) Djelass moins d'un hectolitre, fèves a) Mahdia 6 hectol.; misprinted check figures in blé c) and fèves c).
- **sgt1924**: f1174-f1197 (pp. 184-207), same layout as sgt1922 (31 caïdats; blé a) without Djerid) [transcribed, every cell read on the image]. Faulty cell (total_mismatch): fèves b) Béja 6 hectol.; misprinted check figures in blé b) Totaux and orge c).
- **sgt1925**: f220-f267 (pp. 196-243), two-page spreads (left page: région, contrôle civil, caïdat and the 6 hectol. column; right page: 5 hectol. ... new land), joined as one table per crop and roll; 36 caïdats (Cap-Bon printed in two lines, Nabeul and Soliman) [transcribed, every cell read on the image]. The 6 hectol. figures against the gutter are partly hidden: solved from the TOTAL (derived_from_totals). 18 faulty cells (total_mismatch), many misprinted c) figures.
- **sgt1926**: f776-f783 (pp. 212-219), one page per crop, a) Blé ... h) Lin, européens and indigènes in one table (35 caïdats; Cap-Bon one caïdat) [transcribed, every cell read on the image]. f776 blé: La Skhira européens misprinted (total_mismatch); f779 fèves: Sousse européens, last figure broken, derived from the Récapitulation; f783 lin: Cap-Bon indigènes misprinted (total_mismatch).
- **sgt1927**: f238-f245 (pp. 210-217), same layout, 36 caïdats (Cap-Bon printed as Nabeul and Soliman) [transcribed, every cell read on the image]; every check passes.
- **sgt1928**: f762-f769 (pp. 210-217), same layout as sgt1927 [transcribed, every cell read on the image]. f762 blé: indigènes of Gafsa and Djerid printed one line too high and none for Hemmama (stored on their lines, row_realigned; Hemmama derived from the Récapitulation).
- **sgt1929**: f240-f247 (pp. 212-219), same layout as sgt1927 [transcribed, every cell read on the image]. Faulty cells: avoine Kairouan and pois chiches Mateur indigènes (total_mismatch); misprinted check figures in maïs, sorgho; orge new-land Totaux 5.00 off (unresolved).
- **sgt1930**: f774-f781 (pp. 216-223), same layout as sgt1927 [transcribed, every cell read on the image]. Faulty cell: fèves Aïn-Draham indigènes (total_mismatch); avoine indigènes Totaux misprinted.
- **sgt1935**: f133 (p. 113), ACHOUR (1935), one table, the eight crops, Tunisia totals [transcribed, every cell read on the image].

## Coverage

Every achour table of the ten editions is transcribed, every cell read on native-resolution crops: the yield-class tables of sgt1922 (f188-f211), sgt1923 (f678-f701), sgt1924 (f1174-f1197) and sgt1925 (two-page spreads f220-f267; per crop a) européens, b) indigènes, c) récapitulation x 7 yield classes, TOTAL and the exempted new land; a) and b) stored, TOTAL, Totaux and c) checked), the one-page-per-crop tables of sgt1926-sgt1930 (f776-f783, f238-f245, f762-f769, f240-f247, f774-f781) and sgt1935 (f133).
