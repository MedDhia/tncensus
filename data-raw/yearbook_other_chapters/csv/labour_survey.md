## Coverage

Every labour table of the chapter is transcribed in every edition that prints it, every cell read on native-resolution crops of the page images (whole-page views of every page from the chapter title to the index, then zoomed crops of every table and of every cell behind a failed check). Printed TOTAUX / TOTAL lines are used as checks only. The four agricultural mutual-credit tables printed in sgt1925's chapter (f518-f523: Crédit mutuel agricole, Coopérative centrale des agriculteurs, Assurances mutuelles agricoles, Sociétés indigènes de prévoyance, 1916-1925) are transcribed with the mutual-credit chapter: see mutuality_* (extract_mutuality.py).
