## Columns

- `census`: edition the value is taken from (`sgt1922` … `sgt1939`)
- `table_id`: transcription section (`budget`, `property`, `gas`)
- `gallica_ark`: Gallica volume ark (views are numbered within a volume)
- `source_page`: printed page number
- `gallica_view`: Gallica view (fNNNN)
- `year`: budget year (exercice) / year of the 31 December valuation
- `commune`: commune name exactly as printed in the edition the value is taken from (footnote calls such as `Kram (Le) (1)` kept)
- `measure`: `receipts` (Recettes) or `expenditure` (Dépenses)
- `measure_fr`: column heading as printed
- `value`: francs, centimes kept (two decimals); printed » = 0.00. Gas: consumption in cubic metres (integer), receipts in francs with centimes
- `flag`: empty, `reprint_corrected`, `row_realigned`, `illegible` or `not_applicable` (see below)

Table a) "Budget des communes" of the *Finances municipales* (a two-page spread from sgt1922 to sgt1935, one page in sgt1936 and sgt1939): for every commune, the receipts (*Recettes*) and expenditure (*Dépenses*) of the budget year, for five years (sgt1922–sgt1935) or three years (sgt1936: 1933–1935; sgt1939: 1936–1938). No budget lines are printed, only the two totals per commune. sgt1936 and sgt1939 head the receipts column "Recettes y compris excédents laissés par les exercices antérieurs" (receipts including surpluses carried over from earlier years); the 1933 and 1934 figures are identical in sgt1935 (headed "Recettes") and sgt1936, so the heading changed, not the definition. The printed TOTAUX row is not stored. Printed » is stored as 0.00: it is printed for communes that had no budget yet (e.g. El-Djem, Hadjeb-el-Aïoun, Ksour-Essaf before 1923) or no longer (Kram (Le) from 1926, Pêcherie (La) from 1928) and for the Syndicat in some years.

Years 1931–1932 are printed only in sgt1935 and 1935–1938 only in sgt1936 / sgt1939 (there are no 1931–1934 or 1937–1938 yearbooks in the series).

**Lines printed one row out of place.** (1) In all five printings of 1923 (sgt1923–sgt1927, the same type) the receipts and expenditure from the line of the Syndicat des Communes de la Banlieue Nord de Tunis to the line of Zaghouan belong one line lower: Tozeur's line carries 10.483.423,50 / 7.065.936,84 (Tunis's budget; Tozeur has 125.715 in 1925), Tunis's line 60.716,43, and the line of Zarzis a ».
