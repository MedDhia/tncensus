## Coverage

Transcribed, every printing, every cell read on native-resolution crops of the page images (OCR used only as a cross-check, flag `ocr_corrected`): the two budget tables (finance_budget_receipts, finance_budget_expenditure, sgt1925-sgt1939); the dette tunisienne (finance_public_debt, finance_public_debt_balance, sgt1925-sgt1939; not printed in sgt1922-sgt1924, whose chapters f185-f230, f675-f720 and f1171-f1216 open with the taxe personnelle); the droit de patente by class of profit (finance_patente, sgt1922-sgt1930; the folding plates sgt1929 f259 and sgt1930 f793 read in vertical strips) and by caïdat (finance_patente_caidat, sgt1935, sgt1936, sgt1939); the taxe personnelle (finance_taxe_personnelle, sgt1922-sgt1930); the taxe sur le bétail (finance_taxe_betail, sgt1922-sgt1926: a) rôles européens and b) rôles indigènes stored, c) récapitulation read in full as a check). Not yet transcribed (see the survey above for views): achour (sgt1922-sgt1935), canoun des dattiers and des oliviers (sgt1922-sgt1935). The chapter reprints neither the public assistance / health budgets nor the communal budgets.
