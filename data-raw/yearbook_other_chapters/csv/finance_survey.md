## Coverage

Transcribed so far: the two budget tables (finance_budget_receipts, finance_budget_expenditure), every printing (sgt1925-sgt1939), every cell read on native-resolution crops of the page images. Not yet transcribed (see the survey above for views): dette tunisienne (sgt1925-sgt1939), taxe personnelle (sgt1922-sgt1930), achour (sgt1922-sgt1935), canoun des dattiers and des oliviers (sgt1922-sgt1935), droit de patente (sgt1922-sgt1939), taxe sur le bétail (sgt1922-sgt1926). The chapter reprints neither the public assistance / health budgets nor the communal budgets.
