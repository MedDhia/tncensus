First and last views confirmed on the page images (chapter title page and next chapter title). Extraction: `extract_mutuality.py`. Every transcribed cell was read on native-resolution crops of the page images (whole-table crops at 0.6-0.8 scale, zoomed crops for every doubtful figure and failed check); the Gallica OCR was used only to locate tables.
