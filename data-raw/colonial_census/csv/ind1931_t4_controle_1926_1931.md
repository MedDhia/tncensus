# Tableau IV — Statistique comparée des dénombrements de 1926-1931 concernant la population indigène de la Tunisie répartie par Contrôles Civils

*Table IV: indigenous population by contrôle civil, 1926 and 1931 censuses*

Source: *Dénombrement de la population civile indigène (musulmane et israélite) en Tunisie au 22 mars 1931*, Régence de Tunis, Direction générale de l'intérieur, Tunis, Impr. J. Aloccio, 1932. Gallica ark `bpt6k994559s` (Source gallica.bnf.fr / Bibliothèque nationale de France).

Pages: pp. 98–99, view 54. Table id: `IV`. Rows: 80.

## Content

One row per contrôle civil × religion × census year. The printed 'Augmentation ou diminution' (change) columns and the general totals are not stored; they were checked (|1931 − 1926| = printed change; Muslim + Jewish = general total). 1931 values were also cross-checked against Table I.

Columns: census, table_id, source_page, gallica_view, controle_civil, religion, religion_fr, census_year, value, flag.

## Notes and printed footnotes

Printed note (translated): the Muslim population, 1,932,184 in 1926, increased by 226,967; the Jewish population, 54,243 in 1926, increased by 2,005; the total indigenous population, 1,986,427 in 1926, increased by 228,972. 'This happy result must, without any doubt, be attributed to the improvement of hygiene and of material conditions of existence.' The scan has pencil circles around some Sfax and Tunis figures; the printed values are used.

## Method

Numbers come from Gallica's word OCR (via `ocr.py`), assigned to printed columns by right edge,
with page images used to correct the OCR. Script: `extract_ind1931_other.py`. Printed `»` = 0.
Printed subtotal, total, `A reporter` and `Report` lines are not stored; they were used for validation.
`flag = ocr_corrected` marks a cell where the OCR value was missing or wrong and the value was read
from the page image (an empty OCR cell is set to 0 only where the image shows `»` and the printed
row totals then balance; every such cell was confirmed on the image).
