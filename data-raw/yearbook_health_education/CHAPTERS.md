## Health chapter contents (as in sgt1927; later editions differ)

- **Budget:** a) receipts; b) expenditure; c) credits for child-protection works; d) accounts of the subsidised societies and charities. From sgt1935 the layout is "État des recettes et des dépenses du budget de l'Assistance publique".
- **Hospitals:**
  - Hôpital civil français de Tunis: admissions and discharges by year and sex; from sgt1935, statistics by ward.
  - Hôpital Sadiki: admissions and discharges, 10-year windows; external consultations; patient-days.
  - Hôpital des contagieux de la Rabta: admissions and discharges; diseases treated by nationality.
  - Hospitals and dispensaries of the interior: finances; patients and deaths, European and indigenous.
  - Manouba mental hospital (from sgt1935).
- **Institut Pasteur, Institut Arloing:** budgets; doses of serums and vaccines distributed.
- **Statistique sanitaire de la ville de Tunis:** a) deaths by month and nationality; c) deaths by cause and nationality (and b) where printed).

## Public-instruction chapter contents (as in sgt1927)

- Total pupils, public and private, at 31 December.
- Examinations: candidates by nationality and sex.
- Higher, secondary and special education: establishments with civil personality; pupils by nationality and sex.
- Primary education:
  - a) public primary schools, school by school (locality, school, staff, classes, boys and girls by nationality), grouped by inspection district;
  - b) private schools, school by school;
  - c) vocational education of the indigenous population.
- Comparative statistics: a) school population by type of school × nationality × sex; b) quinquennial series of pupils from 1885 (boys/girls, public/private).
- From sgt1935–1939 some tables are regrouped: libraries, "établissements d'enseignement secondaire, primaire supérieur ou technique", first-degree totals.
