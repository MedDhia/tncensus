# Statistique générale de la Tunisie: Démographie chapter by edition

Each Gallica volume binds one to three annual editions. Each edition opens
with Chapter I, *Démographie*: tables from the latest census (*État de la
population d'après le recensement de …*), then *Mouvement de la population*
(European civil registration; Muslim and Jewish births and deaths), *Expulsions*
and *Naturalisations*.

## Tables seen in the chapter (from the page headings)

- **1921 census (eds 1922–1925):** a) population and density by contrôle civil;
  b) French civilian population by place of birth, censuses 1891–1921;
  c) French by naturalisation; f) Europeans by age, sex and marital status (by
  nationality); h) Europeans by profession and nationality (by sex);
  i) recapitulation of Europeans by place of birth; m) 1911 and 1921 censuses
  by circonscription (Europeans, French population movement); n) Europeans by
  nationality, quinquennial censuses 1881–1921.
- **1926 census (eds 1926–1930):** a) population by contrôle civil (Europeans
  by nationality, Muslims, Jews); b) French by place of birth 1891–1926;
  c) foreigners by place of birth 1921 and 1926; e) Europeans by age, sex and
  marital status; f) Europeans by profession and nationality; g) Europeans by
  instruction 1921 and 1926; i) Europeans and indigenous population of the
  five largest towns; k) every commune: Europeans by nationality, Muslims,
  Jews, decree of creation, contrôle civil.
- **1931 census (ed 1935):** population by contrôle civil; 1926 vs 1931
  Europeans by circonscription and nationality; five largest towns.
- **1936 census (eds 1936, 1939):** population by contrôle civil; 1931 vs 1936
  Europeans by circonscription and nationality; five largest towns.
- **Mouvement de la population (every edition):** European marriages, births
  and deaths (and divorces where printed) by contrôle civil / nationality, for
  the last 2–5 years; Muslim and Jewish births and deaths by contrôle civil,
  last 5 years.
- **Expulsions** by nationality, last 10 years; **Naturalisations**
  (individual naturalisations, civil status, age and profession, nationality of
  the naturalised) for the edition year.
