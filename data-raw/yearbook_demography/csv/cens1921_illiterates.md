# cens1921_illiterates

**i) Nombre et proportion des européens illettrés**  
*Number of illiterate Europeans, 1921*

Source: *Statistique générale de la Tunisie*, 1922 edition (sgt1922), chapter I *Démographie*, printed pages 10, Gallica views 34 of ark `bpt6k9105649x`. Source gallica.bnf.fr / Ministère des Affaires étrangères.

4 rows, one per printed cell. `all_europeans` includes the nationalities not listed. The printed proportions (15,5; 50,3; 50,9; 37,7 %) are not stored.

Common columns: `census` (source edition), `gallica_ark`, `table_id`, `source_page`, `gallica_view`, `census_year`, `value` (printed figure; » = 0), `flag` (see SPEC.md and SPEC_ADDENDUM.md).
