# 1926 census, table k: communes

**k) Population européenne et indigène des Centres de la Régence érigés en Communes**
*European and indigenous population of the 59 communes, with the date of the decree creating
each commune and the contrôle civil it belongs to, 1926 census.*

Source: **sgt1927**, `bpt6k91056510` views 42–45 (printed pp. 14–17), chosen over sgt1926
(`bpt6k9105650k` views 578–581) because the binding hides the *Autres européens* column there.
Values are identical in all printings. Source gallica.bnf.fr / Ministère des Affaires étrangères.

Rows: 59 communes × 8 groups = 472. `decree_date` is ISO (`1858` for Tunis, printed as a year
only); `decree_date_fr` as printed. No printed totals row.

## Method and validation
Read cell by cell on the full-resolution scans of sgt1927 (all 649 printed cells). Row checks
(Europeans, indigenous, total): 177 checks, all pass.

**Cross-check with the 1931 census volume** (Annex to Table VII, 1926 column,
`ind1931_t7annex_communes_1921_1931.csv`, indigenous population): 56 of 57 matching communes
agree. **Gabès:** the yearbook prints 13.849 (11.354 Muslims + 2.495 Jews, the same in table j);
the 1931 volume prints 13.819 (checked on its scan). The two publications differ; the value is
kept as printed here. Kram (Le) and Pêcherie (La) are not in the 1931 annex; the annex lists
Sbeïtla (1926: 0), which is not a commune in this table.

## Reprint comparison
Each printing's OCR tokens were matched to the commune rows, without relying on column
assignment (tokens matching a value of the row / tokens read): sgt1926 537 / 580; sgt1927
624 / 669; sgt1928 569 / 601; sgt1929 567 / 583; sgt1930 591 / 604. All 113 non-matching
tokens were viewed on the scans: OCR misreads, or values of the *Autres européens* column
spilling across the binding. No printing variant was found. Cells that a printing's OCR did
not read at all (about 7 %) were not compared for that printing.
