# 1926 census, table c: foreign population by place of birth, 1921 and 1926

**c) Population étrangère d'après le lieu de naissance (recensements de 1921 et 1926)**
*Foreign (non-French European) population by place of birth, 1921 and 1926 censuses.*

Source: sgt1926, `bpt6k9105650k` view 570 (printed p. 6). Reprinted in sgt1927 (view 34),
sgt1928 (558), sgt1929 (34) and sgt1930 (564). 36 places of birth × 2 censuses = 72 rows.
Footnote (1) to Alsace-Lorraine: included with the French population (hence `»`).

## Method and validation
Transcribed from the scans (sgt1926 and sgt1929). Carried total (`A reporter`
100.003 / 99.803) and grand totals (101.639 / 102.261): 4 checks, all pass. Italy (38.736 /
38.821) equals the grand total of table d.

sgt1930 69 / 0 / 9. Every printed cell was confirmed by at least one printing's OCR except
three, which were read on the scans. All differences were OCR misreads.
