# cens1921_birthplace_recap

**j) Récapitulation de la population européenne d'après le lieu de naissance**  
*Europeans by place of birth: French by birthplace, foreigners born outside North Africa, in Tunisia and in Algeria, 1921*

Source: *Statistique générale de la Tunisie*, 1922 edition (sgt1922), chapter I *Démographie*, printed pages 11, Gallica views 35 of ark `bpt6k9105649x`. Source gallica.bnf.fr / Ministère des Affaires étrangères.

10 rows, one per printed cell. For foreigners, the split by nationality is printed only for those born in Italy, Malta or outside North Africa; births in Tunisia and Algeria are printed for all foreigners together (`all_foreigners`).

Common columns: `census` (source edition), `gallica_ark`, `table_id`, `source_page`, `gallica_view`, `census_year`, `value` (printed figure; » = 0), `flag` (see SPEC.md and SPEC_ADDENDUM.md).
